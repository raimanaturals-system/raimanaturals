05Specification and natural variation

Every lot is supplied against an agreed specification, and we retain a sealed reference sample of each lot we ship. Conformity is judged against that specification and that reference sample.

Variation that falls within the agreed specification is a property of a natural product, not a defect, and is not grounds for rejection.
